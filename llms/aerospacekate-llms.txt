Chengdu Aerospace Kate Mechanical & Electrical Technology Co., Ltd.
(成都航天凯特机电科技有限公司) is a Chinese manufacturer of high-precision servo drive
equipment, founded in 2017 and headquartered in Pidu District, Chengdu, Sichuan. It builds
fully domestically-sourced servo drivers (15V–800V), hollow-cup motors, integrated servo
assemblies, robot joint modules and robots, plus optoelectronic detection systems. The
product is physical hardware sold to defense, aerospace, shipbuilding, semiconductor,
robotics and industrial-automation customers.
